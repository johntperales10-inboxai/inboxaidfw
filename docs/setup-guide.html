What you bought

InboxAI is an assistant that lives inside your own Gmail. Once it's installed, it checks your inbox every hour and uses Google's Gemini AI to decide what to do with each new email:

★ Stars emails from real people and from the senders and organizations you trust.
Clears spam by moving your spam folder to the trash (if you turn this on).
Unsubscribes you from newsletters you've stopped opening.
Asks you by email when it isn't sure, instead of guessing, and learns from your answer.

Nothing is ever permanently deleted. Trashed emails stay in your Gmail trash for 30 days, so you can always get them back.

Premium only

Priority Inbox dashboard. Every unread email gets an importance score from 0–100 and is sorted into what needs you now, today, this week, and what can wait. See page 4.

Get back to setup any time

Go to inboxaidfw.com, click Sign in to set up, and sign in with the same Google account you used at checkout. Your purchase is linked to that email.

Step 1: Sign in and fill out the form

  1. After you pay, you'll see a “You're all set!” page. Click Sign in to set up → and choose your Google account. (Closed the page? Just go to inboxaidfw.com and sign in.)
  2. Get your free AI key. Click Get my free Gemini key →. On Google AI Studio: sign in with Google → Get API Key → Create API key → copy it. Come back and paste it in the box (it starts with AIzaSy).
  3. Star whole organizations (optional): add domains like @yourschool.org or @company.com. Every email from them gets starred.
  4. Who do you always want to hear from? Add at least one email address you trust, like a teacher, boss or family member. Their emails always get starred.
  5. Newsletter cleanup: choose how long a newsletter can go unopened before InboxAI unsubscribes you.
  6. Auto-delete spam: turn it on to move everything in your spam folder to the trash every hour.
  7. Notification email and preference: where InboxAI should write to you, and how often:
    • Only when action needed (recommended): it only writes when it finds something it isn't sure about.
    • Daily summary: one message a day with everything it did.
    • Every run: a message every hour (not recommended).
Keep your AI key private. It's baked into your personal script, so don't share your script with anyone.

Step 2: Install your script

When you finish the form, the site builds your own ready-to-use script with your settings already inside. Click Copy my ready-to-use script, then:

  1. Go to script.google.com and sign in with the same Google account as your Gmail. Click New project.
  2. Delete everything in the editor. Paste your script with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac). Save with Ctrl+S (Cmd+S).
  3. At the top of the editor there's a dropdown that says myFunction. Change it to testGemini and press ▶ Run. Accept the permissions Google asks for. This checks that your AI key works.
  4. Change the dropdown to setupTrigger and press ▶ Run. Your agent is now live and runs every hour by itself. You'll get a “Gmail Agent active!” email.
“Google hasn't verified this app”? Because the script is yours and runs only in your account, Google may show this warning the first time. Click Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe), then Allow. The script only runs in your own account, and nobody else sees your email.

After setup: answering your agent

When InboxAI isn't sure about an email, it leaves it alone and sends you a “email(s) need your attention” message with a numbered list. Just reply to that email with simple commands. It reads your reply on its next hourly run.

Reply withWhat happens
TRASH 1Trashes email #1
STAR 2Stars email #2
UNSUB 3Unsubscribes you from email #3's sender
IGNORE 1Leaves email #1 alone
TRASH 1, UNSUB 2, STAR 3Does several at once

Your agent learns from your answers

Every answer becomes a rule, so InboxAI stops asking about the same thing. Add a word to choose how far the rule reaches:

Reply withRule it learns
TRASH 1For that exact sender (the default)
TRASH 1 DOMAINFor everyone at that sender's domain
UNSUB 2 TYPEFor that kind of email (unsubscribe request, meeting request, pricing question…)
TRASH 3 ONCEJust this once, no rule learned

See or delete what it learned

  • Reply RULES to any agent email: it emails you the full list with rule IDs.
  • Reply FORGET R123abc to delete one rule, or FORGET ALL to delete them all.
  • Or, in script.google.com, run showRules, deleteRule or resetRules from the dropdown.
Premium only

Open your Priority Inbox

Your Premium script includes the Priority Inbox dashboard. It runs inside your own Google account, so your email never passes through our servers. Make sure your Gemini key is filled in, then publish it as a private web app (about 2 minutes):

  1. In the same script editor, click the blue Deploy button (top right) → New deployment.
  2. Click the gear ⚙ next to “Select type” and choose Web app.
  3. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Only myself. Click Deploy, then Authorize access if Google asks.
  4. Copy the Web app URL. That's your private Priority Inbox. Bookmark it, or save it at inboxaidfw.com/dashboard so it's one click away.

Changed your script later? Use Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit (pencil icon) → Version: New version → Deploy, so the dashboard keeps the same address.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Sign-in says it can't find your purchaseIt can take up to a minute for a purchase to register. Wait a moment and try again.
Still can't find itMake sure you're signed in with the Google account for the email you used at checkout. If that email isn't a Google account, email us and we'll move your purchase over.
testGemini shows an errorYour AI key may be missing or copied wrong. Go back to inboxaidfw.com, sign in, paste the key again, and copy the new script into your project.
Nothing happens after setupMake sure you ran setupTrigger, not just testGemini. You should have received a “Gmail Agent active!” email.
It trashed something it shouldn't haveRestore it from Gmail's trash (emails stay there for 30 days). To stop it happening again, add the sender to your trusted list and rebuild your script.

Pause or remove InboxAI

  • Pause: in script.google.com, open your project, choose stopAgent from the dropdown and press ▶ Run. This stops the hourly runs. Run setupTrigger again to restart.
  • Remove completely: run stopAgent, then go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove your script's access. You can also delete the project in script.google.com.
